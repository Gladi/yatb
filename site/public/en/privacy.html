Privacy policy

This page explains which data the YATB website and the YATB Discord bot process, why, and for how long.

1. Who is responsible

Contact details of the operator will be added here.

2. This website

  • The information pages use no cookies, no tracking, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts.
  • To deliver the pages, your browser's IP address and request are processed briefly by the web server. The web server does not keep access logs.
  • The website runs on a server of Oracle Cloud in Frankfurt (Germany). HTTPS certificates come from Let's Encrypt; no visitor data is shared for this.
  • Links to other sites (Discord, Ko-fi, providers) are normal links - their privacy policies apply there.

Dashboard (login with Discord)

  • At /app you can log in with Discord. YATB only receives your Discord user ID and name (scope identify). Discord's access token is revoked right after login and never stored.
  • A session cookie (yatb_session, valid 7 days) keeps you logged in; during login a second cookie protects against forged logins for at most 10 minutes. Both are technically necessary and are not used for anything else.
  • Stored on our server: a hash of the session token, your Discord ID, display name, language and the expiry date - deleted when you log out or after 7 days.
  • Whether you may manage a server is checked by the bot directly with Discord on every request. Changes you make are reported in that server's log channel with your name.

4. Translation services

The admins of each server decide which services translate their messages. Messages of members with Local only are only translated by LibreTranslate on our own server. On servers run by the operator, the operator's own paid accounts are used.

5. Discord

Messages are written and shown on Discord. Discord processes them under its own privacy policy.

6. Legal basis

Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - our legitimate interest, and the interest of the server communities, in multilingual communication and in a secure, reliable service. Your choice of Local only limits the processing to our own server.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object, and you can complain to a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, contact the operator (section 1). In Discord, /yatb-user privacy lets you choose Local only at any time.
